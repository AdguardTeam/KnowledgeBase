:::note

This page is about AdGuard Mini for Android, which safeguards Samsung Browser and Yandex Browser and provides DNS protection. To protect your Android device entirely and get more advanced settings, [download the AdGuard app](https://agrd.io/download-kb-adblock).

*Statistics* shows an overview of what AdGuard Mini for Android has blocked.

![Statistics](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/release_notes/ad_blocker/mini_android/v3/statistics.png)

*Recent activity* lists the domains your device has connected to. It helps you spot unwanted domains: if you see one, you can block it right from the list (available in the full version).

![Recent activity](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/release_notes/ad_blocker/mini_android/v3/recent_activity.png)
