:::info

This page is about AdGuard Mini for Android, which safeguards Samsung Browser and Yandex Browser and provides DNS protection. To protect your Android device entirely and get more advanced settings, [download AdGuard for Android](https://agrd.io/download-kb-adblock).

:::

## Samsung Browser

- Make sure AdGuard is selected as the ad blocker in Samsung Browser: open the browser menu, tap *Ad blockers*, and check that AdGuard is toggled on.

- Make sure the necessary filters are enabled in AdGuard Mini: go to *Protection* → *Browser protection* → *Filters* and check that *Ad blocking* and your *Language-specific* filters are turned on.

- Check for filter updates: on the app’s main screen, tap the update icon in the top-right corner.

## Yandex Browser

- Make sure AdGuard is enabled in Yandex Browser: open the browser menu, go to *Settings* → *Content blocking extensions*, and check that AdGuard is toggled on.

- Make sure the necessary filters are enabled in AdGuard Mini: go to *Protection* → *Browser protection* → *Filters* and check that *Ad blocking* and your *Language-specific* filters are turned on.

- Check for filter updates: on the app’s main screen, tap the update icon in the top-right corner.

If the problem persists, go to *Settings* → *Support* → *Report incorrect blocking*, and we’ll look into it.
