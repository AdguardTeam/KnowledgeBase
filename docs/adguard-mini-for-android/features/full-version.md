:::note

This page is about AdGuard Mini for Android, which safeguards Samsung Browser and Yandex Browser and provides DNS protection. To protect your Android device entirely and get more advanced settings, [download the AdGuard app](https://agrd.io/download-kb-adblock).

:::

The core features of AdGuard Mini for Android are free:

- Protection in Samsung Browser and Yandex Browser
- DNS protection
- Video browser
- Statistics and Recent activity

The full version unlocks advanced features:

- [Tracking protection](https://adguard.com/kb/general/stealth-mode/): filters that protect your online activity and personal data from web trackers
- [Custom filters](https://filterlists.com/): add any filter list by URL, in addition to the built-in ones
- [DNS filters]: block more unwanted domains at the DNS level
- Domain management: block and allow domains right from *Recent activity*

The list of advanced features will continue to grow.

## How to get the full version

There are two ways to purchase a subscription:

### On Google Play

You can purchase a subscription right in the app: *Settings* → *License* → *Get full version*. You'll be taken to a page with three subscription types:

- Personal (3 devices)
- Family (9 devices)
- Mobile (1 device) – only available via [Google Play](https://agrd.io/adguard-mini-for-android)

To use the license on other devices, bind it to your AdGuard account: go to *Settings* → *License* → *Bind license*, then log in to that account in other AdGuard Ad Blocker apps.

### On the AdGuard website

On the [official website](https://adguard.com/license.html) you can purchase a yearly or a lifetime license:

- Personal (3 devices)
- Family (9 devices)

The license is universal: it can be used across all supported AdGuard Ad Blocker apps. It’s bound to your AdGuard account automatically.
