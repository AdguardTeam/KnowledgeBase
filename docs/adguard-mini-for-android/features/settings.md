The *Settings* tab contains general app preferences: appearance, filter updates, diagnostics, and support options.

![Settings](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/release_notes/ad_blocker/mini_android/v3/settings.png)

## General

Manage the app’s look and functionality. Here you can change the app’s *Theme* (light, dark, or system default) and set up *Filter updates*. In *Advanced* settings, you can select the *Logging level*, *Export logs*, and turn *Send anonymized app usage data* and *Send crash reports automatically* on or off.
